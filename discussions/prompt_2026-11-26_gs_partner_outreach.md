## Discussion (outcomes)

### Positioning

- **Product gap:** not another climate chatbot; **curated, citable knowledge packs** that an interest group assembles and citizens/field staff use **offline**, without AI literacy or cloud LLMs.
- **GS centre of gravity:** prefer **local/regional scholarship** (theses, university repos, national journals, agency guidance) over Northern OA as the default corpus; IPCC/WMO only where they reinforce local evidence.
- **Model:** interest-group-as-librarian — collect once, package for trust and reuse, deploy offline. Sits between paper participatory manuals and cloud AI.

### Unmet demand (GS-weighted)

1. Municipal / district evidence desks with little specialist capacity  
2. NGO/CSO knowledge stewards who already run participatory NRM/adaptation  
3. Field / low-connectivity deployment (portable corpus + glossary)  
4. Civic scrutiny that must cite named documents  
5. University–civil society bridges for thesis/OA collections (India, LatAm)

### Planned resource base (illustrative)

| Region | Inputs |
|--------|--------|
| India | Shodhganga / university theses; ICAR, IMD, state guidance; Indian OA / institutional repos |
| Latin America | National thesis banks; SciELO; CEPAL / national agencies; university DSpace/OAI |
| Cross-cutting | Selected IPCC/WMO excerpts only as support, not default |
