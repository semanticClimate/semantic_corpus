## Prompt (verbatim)

Corpus will provide definitions for all modules.

Paragraph: 
A chunk of text without natural internal structure other than sentences. This can be:
- a paragraph
- a figure caption
- a table caption
- a list element 
  (please suggest any I have missed)

A paragraph will be semantically definable by an HTML element and will be indexable uniquely within the document. 

Please suggest other segments that might be labelled paragraph and also whether there is a better name.
