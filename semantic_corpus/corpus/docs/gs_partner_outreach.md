# Global South partners — summary & one-page outreach

**Audience:** semanticClimate / semantic_corpus partnership conversations  
**Emphasis:** India & Latin America; theses + scholarly OA; offline, no required online LLMs  
**Date:** 2026-11-26

---

## Summary

**Offer.** Interest groups curate **trustable GS resources** (theses, OA papers, agency guidance—e.g. Shodhganga, SciELO, national repos) into offline **indexed bags** and citizen-facing tools. Citizens and field staff use them **without AI expertise** and **without cloud LLMs** (cost, policy, or field connectivity).

**Gap.** Participatory manuals and geospatial apps exist; few turn **local scholarship → citable, offline decision aids**. Northern assessments stay too coarse; GS theses stay siloed in PDFs.

**Who to partner with.** (1) Field CSOs that already plan with communities (FES, PRADAN, WASSAN/RRA, BAIF…). (2) Knowledge brokers that scale across India & Andean LatAm (CDKN, FFLA, Water Funds). (3) Repository / open-science allies that supply literature, not end UX.

**Pilot sequence.** FES or PRADAN + one Indian thesis/OA contact → prove “theses → offline pack → workshop”; then CDKN/FFLA for a Spanish Water Fund or municipal pack; then WASSAN/RRA to multiply across a CSO network.

**One-line pitch.** *We provide the offline, citation-first literature layer from India/LatAm scholarship; you provide domain curation, field access, and non-expert delivery—neither side needs to become an online-LLM product.*

## One-page outreach

### Subject (email)

`Offline literature packs from local theses/papers — pilot with your field teams (no cloud LLM required)`

### Opening paragraph (reuse)

We build open tooling so civil-society groups can **collect scholarly and authority sources** (including **Indian and Latin American theses and OA papers**), package them for **offline use**, and hand citizens and facilitators tools that always **cite the underlying document**. The stack is designed for **climate and sustainability decision support** where online LLMs are too costly, disallowed, or unavailable in the field. We are looking for **one or two pilot partners** to curate a small topical pack and try it in an existing workshop or planning process.

### What we ask / what we offer

| We ask | We offer |
|--------|----------|
| Topic + geography for a 1-pack pilot (e.g. drought/water in one district; watershed EbA) | Pipeline: ingest → indexed text → offline bag / glossary-style look-up |
| Help select ~20–50 trustable local sources (theses, OA papers, agency notes) | Citation-first UX; runnable without cloud LLMs |
| One facilitated session or field trial with your staff | Joint learning note; no lock-in; open methods |

### Suggested first three emails (this month)

1. **FES** — India Observatory / partnerships: “offline scholarly layer beside your geospatial commons tools.”  
2. **CDKN Asia + CDKN LatAm** — same week: ask for intro to one India CSO and one Water Fund.  
3. **WASSAN** — network magnification once a single-pack pilot is defined.

### Do not lead with

- “AI chatbot” or cloud LLM demos  
- Replacing their participatory or GIS tools  
- Large multi-country proposals before one pack works

### Success criteria for a pilot (write into the ask)

- Pack built from **≥70% GS sources** (theses/OA/agency of India or LatAm)  
- Usable **offline** by a non-technical facilitator  
- Every answer or glossary entry **links to a named document**  
- Short partner note: what was useful / what blocked adoption
